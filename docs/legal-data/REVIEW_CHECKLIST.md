# Legal and RTI Review Checklist

Use this checklist for every jurisdiction, protected template, and legal-data release.

## Source authority

- [ ] Every operative claim uses a primary official source.
- [ ] The issuing authority, document title, publication date, effective date and provision are recorded.
- [ ] The raw source checksum and retrieval metadata are recorded where a file exists.
- [ ] Later amendments, corrigenda, repeals and commencement notifications were searched.
- [ ] Legacy state law is classified and cannot enter the current RTI Act workflow.

## Jurisdiction behavior

- [ ] Central Government and GNCT Delhi are independently routed.
- [ ] Application fee and BPL exemption/evidence rules are exact.
- [ ] Online and offline payment methods are exact and scoped to the filing channel.
- [ ] Portal authority coverage, text limits, attachment format/size and ID warnings are exact.
- [ ] Prescribed versus guidance-only forms are distinguished.
- [ ] Application and first-appeal fees, routes, forms and required attachments are exact.
- [ ] PIO/FAA office, designation and address come from current official sources.
- [ ] No personal officer name is required or stored by default.

## Statutory timing

- [ ] Section 7(1) standard receipt-based period is represented accurately.
- [ ] Section 19(1) first-appeal trigger and filing period are represented accurately.
- [ ] Section 19(6) disposal language, including extension conditions, is represented accurately.
- [ ] Section 7(6) is not overstated or applied outside its terms.
- [ ] Exceptional timing cases are detected and excluded from the MVP calculator.

## Privacy amendment and officer clause

- [ ] DPDP Section 44(3) commencement date is correct.
- [ ] Current Section 8(1)(j) wording is correct.
- [ ] Section 8(2) and other relevant current provisions are not omitted from the legal characterization.
- [ ] The product does not state that official identity is always exempt or always disclosable.
- [ ] The officer clause is optional, unchecked, separate and caveated.

## Template quality

- [ ] Requests seek identifiable existing records rather than explanations or grievance resolution.
- [ ] Default bundle includes action-taken report, file notings, movement records, relevant orders and correspondence.
- [ ] Claims and prayers do not exceed reviewed authority.
- [ ] English and Hindi protected texts are semantically aligned.
- [ ] A proficient Hindi reviewer approves the Hindi text.
- [ ] Disclaimer and provenance guidance remain outside the concise submitted application.

## Approval

- [ ] Automated schema, source and regression checks pass.
- [ ] Technical maintainer approval is recorded.
- [ ] Designated legal/RTI reviewer approval is recorded.
- [ ] Review date and next six-month freshness date are recorded.
- [ ] Application and legal-data versions are assigned.
